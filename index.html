THE ORIGINAL IDEA

Your schedule should be adjustable.

A calendar usually treats every busy block as a wall. The idea behind Reflow is to treat a schedule as a living arrangement: some commitments stay fixed, others can move within agreed boundaries.

When something important arrives, rearrange the flexible parts, explain the trade-offs, and automatically notify the people affected. If someone needs a different time, let them ask—and reflow the plan again.

Rearrange the schedule.
Respect the people in it.

    Apply only the agreed revision. Real calendar syncing and email delivery are future integrations.

What works today—and what is simulated?

The scheduling engine, constraint editing, before/after comparison, local saves, JSON import/export, participant responses, revision checks, and notification previews work in this browser. All people and example events are fictional. There is no shared backend, email delivery, push notification, account login, or Google/Outlook calendar connection. Participant actions are role-play controls, not authenticated responses.

How does Reflow choose a plan?

It searches a single 09:00–18:00 day on a 15-minute grid. Fixed events never move. Equal and higher priorities are protected by default. Every existing event keeps its duration. Moving a meeting costs more than moving a flexible task. The lowest-cost plan is selected when the search completes; a search limit is clearly reported when it does not.

Movement score = priority × type weight × (4 + minutes moved / 15). Type weight is 3 for movable meetings and 1 for flexible tasks. No movement costs 0. This is an illustrative comparison score, not money or a validated human disruption metric. The engine allows up to 12 total events and 250,000 search nodes.

Where is my data stored?

Your saved day and draft request stay in this browser’s local storage. Notification previews and approvals last only for this page session. Nothing you enter is sent to a server by the app. Export your plan before clearing browser storage or switching devices. Hosting infrastructure may process normal request logs.
